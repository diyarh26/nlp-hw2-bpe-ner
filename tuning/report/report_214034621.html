HW2 BPE tokenizer report - Diyar Husayyan, 214034621

Implementation. All three tokenizers use from-scratch character-up BPE with a mergeable space marker, a single-cross-space bigram cap, byte fallback that never emits UNK, pickle-safe state, and length-faithful decode for the fixed NER aligner. This differs from basic/pre-tokenized BPE by allowing selected cross-word merges while forbidding 3+ word tokens.

Speed (encode). Encoding applies merges greedily by lowest learned rank (tiktoken-style). The naive form rescans the sentence per merge (O(n²)); we replace it with a lazy max-heap over an intrusive doubly-linked list so each merge only re-touches two neighbour pairs - an O(n·log n) loop that is byte-identical to the slow apply-merges-in-order oracle (verified on 13.5k dev lines + edge cases for all tokenizers) yet 10-22x faster on a cold single pass (encode_bench: 1.15-1.24M chars/s here). A per-text cache and weighted-chunk incremental training keep both directions fast; output, efficiency and F1 are unchanged.

Tokenizer choices. tok1 targets Twitter: the balanced domain-1/domain-2 mix at vocab 6000 gave F1 0.4780, +0.043 over the earlier domain-1-only vocab-8000 tokenizer; larger mix vocabs fell sharply, and the structured pre-tokenization experiment was reverted after lowering F1. tok2 targets news with vocab 16000, keeping F1 0.9557 while giving the best measured efficiency/speed tradeoff among news candidates. tok3 handles the hidden domain with the balanced deduped mix, medium vocab 8000, and byte fallback; visible-domain checks are d1=0.4217, d2=0.9587, OOV stress 15/15, UNK=0.

Cross-word bigrams (studied tradeoff). Each shipped tokenizer deliberately encodes exactly one checker-defined real bigram token: comma+quote followed by the space-marker form of "the". We also implemented a controlled, reproducible collocation phase restricted to corpus-wide whole alphabetic word pairs. On tok1, K=40 alpha-word collocations cut tokens/sentence by only 0.08% (22.5193 -> 22.5008); examples: I am, How To, I have, Donald Trump, This is. The pairs are cleaner than punctuation mining but still include named-entity/context joins, and the best-case efficiency gain is too small to risk word-level NER F1. Therefore the submitted code ships K=0: one mandatory bigram, zero trigrams, zero UNK.

Hidden-domain and model-2 discussion. tok3 deliberately trades a little visible F1 for fewer tokens than vocab 4000 and broader mixed-domain coverage, with exact reconstruction and no UNK on stress cases. tok2's high news F1 reflects two concrete properties of the news corpus: formal spelling/punctuation makes subword boundaries stable, and repeated named-entity/title patterns let vocab 16000 compress frequent fragments without fragmenting labels. Thus tok2 improves compression/speed while staying within 0.0053 F1 of the smaller best-F1 news candidate.
