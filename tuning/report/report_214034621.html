HW2 BPE tokenizer report - Diyar Husayyan, 214034621

Implementation. All three tokenizers use from-scratch character-up BPE with a mergeable space marker S, one-cross-space bigram cap, byte fallback that never emits UNK, pickle-safe state, and length-faithful decode for the fixed NER aligner. This differs from basic/pre-tokenized BPE by allowing selected cross-word merges while forbidding 3+ word tokens.

Tokenizer choices. tok1 targets Twitter: the balanced domain-1/domain-2 mix at vocab 6000 gave F1 0.4780, +0.043 over the earlier domain-1-only vocab-8000 tokenizer; larger mix vocabs fell sharply, and the structured pre-tokenization experiment was reverted after lowering F1. tok2 targets news with vocab 16000, keeping F1 0.9557 while giving the best measured efficiency/speed tradeoff among news candidates. tok3 handles the hidden domain with the balanced deduped mix, medium vocab 8000, and byte fallback; visible-domain checks are d1=0.4217, d2=0.9587, OOV stress 15/15, UNK=0.
