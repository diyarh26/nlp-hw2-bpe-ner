## Phase Goal

Build tokenizer 3 on a deliberate, balanced, deduped domain-1+2 mix so the hidden-domain tokenizer can be regenerated and evaluated from auditable evidence.

    - `train_commands.txt` documents the mix helper command that reproduces the tok3 mix.
  </truths>
  <prohibitions>

  </prohibitions>
</must_haves>
